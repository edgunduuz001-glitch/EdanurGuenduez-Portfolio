Selected work / Foody

FOOD DISCOVERY · MOBILE UX/UI CONCEPT

Good food.

Better company.

Recipes, cooking workshops and food buddies in one mobile experience.

MY ROLE

UX/UI Designer

MY FOCUS

Concept &

UX/UI Design

CONTEXT

Mobile App

WHEN

2024

> 01 / THE DESIGN CHALLENGE

Finding a recipe

is only the beginning.

Foody brings three related activities into one experience: discovering food, learning to cook and sharing a meal. The design challenge was to make each path easy to find without crowding the first screen.

The home screen gives Recipes, Buddies and Workshops separate entry points. Saved recipes and Ask AI support the journey when users need inspiration or cooking guidance.

> 02 / A CONNECTED VISUAL LANGUAGE

Cook something.

Learn something.

Meet someone.

Three clear starting points support different intentions. Each leads into a focused flow, while a shared navigation and visual language connect the experience.

Recipes / Discover what to cook

Workshops / Learn together

Buddies / Share a meal

> 02B / AI COOKING ASSISTANT

A cooking question.

A clearer next step.

Ask AI brings cooking questions into the same experience. Users can describe what they need, then read a focused response without leaving Foody.

Ask a question / Describe what you need

Read the answer / Continue cooking

> 03 / DISCOVER

From a craving

to a cooking plan.

A visual recipe grid makes browsing quick. The detail screen brings preparation time, servings, ingredients and instructions together so users can move from inspiration to the task.

Show food first, then make the practical details easy to find.

> 04 / JOIN

Find a workshop.

Know the next step.

Location, date and dietary filters narrow the choice. The booking screen gathers the workshop, participant count and total before confirmation; a success state closes the flow.

Separate choosing, reviewing and confirming a workshop.

> 05 / CONNECT

A shared appetite.

A conversation starter.

Food-buddy cards start with what someone wants to eat. An invitation can lead into a chat with the meeting context visible, helping the connection continue beyond discovery.

Carry the food and meeting context into the conversation.

> 06 / A FAMILIAR EXPERIENCE, IN TWO MODES

Warm in daylight.

Clear after dark.

Light and dark versions keep the same hierarchy and navigation. Shared colour variables connect the screens, so the visual system can change appearance without changing the journey.

Light / A warm starting point

Dark / The same familiar structure

> 07 / PROJECT SUMMARY

Three ways to begin.

One connected food experience.

My contribution

I designed the mobile interface around recipes, workshops and food-buddy discovery, with supporting booking, conversation and appearance states.

The experience

The concept connects discovery with clear next actions. It demonstrates the proposed journeys and visual system, rather than a launched food service.
