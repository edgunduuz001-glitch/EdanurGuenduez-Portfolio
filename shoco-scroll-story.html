Selected work / ShoCo

SHOPPING COMPANIONS · GROUP PROJECT

Less waiting.

More playing.

A checkout divider. A small moment of play. A collection that continues on your phone.

MY ROLE

UX/UI Designer

MY FOCUS

Group Project

CONTEXT

Game Design

WHEN

Summer Semester 2026

> 01 / THE STARTING QUESTION

You’re waiting.

What if you could

play instead?

We turned a familiar checkout object into a starting point for play. I designed the app journey, visual language and digital reward mechanics.

I researched waiting-time perception and shopper behaviour, designed the app’s UX/UI architecture and visual language, and developed the digital card-game and reward mechanics.

> 02 / SCAN

A twist.

A match.

A new start.

Matching the blocks reveals a code. Scanning leads user the physical puzzle into the app.

A clear scanning step connects the two touchpoints.

> 03 / REVEAL

That small win?

a little moment of delight

A new character card appears. Keep winning these collectable cards and get the big reward.

A distinct card-reveal state makes the collection tangible.

> 04 / COMPLETE

One card becomes

a collection.

Three cards from one category unlock a coupon. The deck view gives every card a place and makes progress visible.

Category-based sets create a clear collecting goal.

> 05 / FROM COLLECTION TO REWARD

A familiar metaphor.

Works digitally.

The coupon borrows a familiar paper gesture: tear to activate. Separate viewing and activation states make the next step clear.

> 06 / PLAY BEYOND CHECKOUT

Keep playing

beyond checkout.

Pocket Battles gives collected cards a second purpose. I designed a simple card game against AI: players choose a card without seeing the AI’s choice, and the higher-powered card wins. Nutri-Score determines card power.

> 07 / PROJECT SUMMARY

A small interaction.

A connected experience.

My contribution

I designed the app UX/UI, collection and coupon flows, and digital game and reward interactions within our team concept.

The experience

A physical puzzle leads into a digital collection, usable coupons and card battles. The project brings these touchpoints together in a Figma prototype.
