About Me

A little about me

Hi, I’m Edanur. I’m a UX/UI Designer studying for a master’s in Design and Interaction at Hochschule Rhein-Waal.

I enjoy creating colorful and thoughtful solutions and experiences. I love it when design connects the digital and physical worlds. The world is becoming increasingly digital and automated, and I’m drawn to explore this new world through analog objects and tactile interactions, taking inspiration from the past. I believe that, in this way, I can embrace the possibilities of the future. I’m following the new developments in AI and how to use AI meaningfully in my products.

My work mostly focuses on websites, mobile apps, and game interfaces. Besides these interests, I’m also pursuing the design of spatial installations and posters, as well as drawing. Beyond my passion for design, I love video games, board games, books, comics, and manga. My hobbies are the most important things that give me inspiration for my projects.
