> 01 / THE DESIGN CHALLENGE

Many attributes.

One small canvas.

For an unannounced card game, I designed a layout that organises complex information while keeping the artwork prominent. The focus was on clear hierarchy and consistency across different card types.

My role focused on information hierarchy and card layout.

> 02 / AN UNITED VISUAL LANGUAGE

Same structure.

Different roles.

The selected designs use different frame colours for Light, Heavy, Structure, Director, Support and Equipment cards. Shared positions connect the family; colour helps distinguish each role.

> 03 / IDENTIFY

Know the card.

Before reading the rules.

Name and classification sit above the artwork. Tier and cost occupy the upper-left corner, giving the player a repeatable place to look first.

Separate card identity from its effect text.

> 04 / PLAY

Read the action.

Keep the numbers close.

Combat values and trait badges sit at the artwork’s edge. Effect text has its own area below, while target and production requirements sit along the bottom.

Group information by the moment it is needed.

> 05 / ADAPT

A shared language.

Room for different cards.

Structure and Director cards use the same identity and artwork hierarchy. Their remaining space accommodates production rules or supporting text without forcing every card to show the same combat fields.

Keep the family recognisable while allowing different content.

> 06 / FROM SKETCH TO CARD

The rules set the brief.

The layout gives them order.

I explored different ways to organise card information. In the final layout, I gave the artwork more space while keeping key details clear and consistent.

> 07 / PROJECT SUMMARY

Designed to be recognised.

Structured to be read.

My contribution

I designed the card interface and explored how hierarchy, colour and repeated positions could support a complex trading card game.

The experience

The selected card layouts demonstrate the visual family. This case study focuses on the interface; exploring ways of visualize something complex.
